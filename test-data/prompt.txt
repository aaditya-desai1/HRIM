2)  4-WEEK VEGAN MEAL PLAN
Structure: 
- Create FOUR SEPARATE COMPLETE tables - one for each week
- Label each section clearly: ## Week 1, ## Week 2, ## Week 3, ## Week 4
- DO NOT use phrases like "Weeks 2-4 follow similar structure" - generate unique tables for all weeks

For each week's table:
- Columns: Day (Mon–Sun), Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner
- Each meal must include:
  * Dish name
  * Calories (kcal)
  * Macronutrients: Carbs (C), Protein (P), Fat (F), Fiber (Fi) (in g)

Example format:
| Day | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Dish (150 kcal, C:20g, P:8g, F:5g, Fi:4g) | Dish (300 kcal, C:40g, P:12g, F:10g, Fi:8g) | Dish (350 kcal, C:45g, P:15g, F:10g, Fi:6g) |

Constraints:
- Quick to prepare (<30 mins)
- Local ingredients
- Use turmeric, cumin, coriander, garam masala, chaat masala, etc if in India.
- Provide quantities for 1-person and Family (4 servings)
- Promote mindful eating (75% fullness)
- Meal Type to be analyzed from the client data and provide meal plan accordingly (Like Veg, Vegan, Non-Veg or Mixed Diet, etc.)
- Eggs are considered Non-veg
