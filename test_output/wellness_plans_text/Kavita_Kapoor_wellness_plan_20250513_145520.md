## 3. DOs and DON'Ts

**DOs:**

* Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily.
* Eat regular, balanced meals.
* Incorporate strength training exercises 2-3 times a week (consult a trainer).
* Practice stress-reducing techniques like yoga, meditation, or deep breathing.
* Get 7-8 hours of quality sleep.
* Maintain a dust-free environment.
* Read food labels carefully to avoid gluten.
* Gradually increase exercise intensity and duration.
* Track your food intake and weight progress.
* Consult a doctor or registered dietitian for personalized guidance.

**DON'Ts:**

* Skip meals.
* Consume processed foods, sugary drinks, and excessive caffeine.
* Stay sedentary for long periods.
* Neglect sleep hygiene.
* Ignore stress management.
* Expose yourself to dust.
* Consume gluten-containing foods.
* Rush into intense exercise without proper preparation.
